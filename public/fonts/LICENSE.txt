Fonts in this folder are subsets of:
- Inter and Inter Display, by Rasmus Andersson — SIL Open Font License 1.1 (https://rsms.me/inter/, https://openfontlicense.org)
- Lora, by Cyreal — SIL Open Font License 1.1 (https://github.com/cyrealtype/Lora-Cyrillic, https://openfontlicense.org)
They are self-hosted so that no visitor request is ever sent to a third-party font service.
